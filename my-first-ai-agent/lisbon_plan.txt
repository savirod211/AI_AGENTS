# 3-Day Weekend Trip to Lisbon

## Budget Breakdown
- Trip Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights
- Accommodation: 2 nights @ €110/night = €220.00
- Food & Dining: 3 days @ €45/day = €135.00
- Total Estimated Cost: €355.00

---

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Historic Heart & Alfama
- **Morning:** Arrive and head to Baixa. Walk through Praça do Comércio along the Tagus riverfront, then take iconic Tram 28 up the hills.
- **Afternoon:** Wander the winding alleys of Alfama, explore São Jorge Castle (Castelo de São Jorge), and enjoy sweeping city views from Miradouro das Portas do Sol.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a traditional Portuguese dinner accompanied by live Fado music in Alfama.

### Day 2: Belém & Vibrant Nightlife
- **Morning:** Take a short tram ride to the Belém district. Tour the Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower, followed by warm custard tarts from Pastéis de Belém.
- **Afternoon:** Stroll along the waterfront, visit MAAT, and stop by the trendy shops and galleries at LX Factory.
- **Evening:** Catch the sunset at Miradouro de Santa Catarina, then head into Bairro Alto and Cais do Sodré (Pink Street) for dinner, petiscos (tapas), and nightlife.

### Day 3: Chiado, Carmo & Panoramic Views
- **Morning:** Explore the elegant Chiado neighborhood. Visit Livraria Bertrand (the world's oldest operating bookstore) and the open-air gothic ruins of Carmo Convent.
- **Afternoon:** Ride the Santa Justa Lift, take in views from Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara, and pick up local souvenirs (ceramics, cork products, tinned sardines).
- **Evening:** Final dinner with river views at Ribeira das Naus before departing.
